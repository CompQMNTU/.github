# Kao Group

The Kao Group is a research group in the Department of Physics at National Taiwan
University. We use computational and analytical methods to study strongly
correlated many-body systems, focusing on emergent phenomena, exotic phases, and
phase transitions. Learn more on our [group website](https://yingjerkao.gitlab.io).

## Research

- **Tensor networks:** We develop and apply tensor-network methods to study
  quantum many-body systems, including frustrated magnets and dissipative
  quantum lattices.
- **Frustrated magnetism:** We investigate classical and quantum spin systems,
  including spin ice and spin liquids, using computational approaches such as
  Monte Carlo simulations.
- **Quantum phase transitions:** We study phase behavior and critical
  phenomena in low-dimensional and strongly correlated systems.
- **Machine learning:** We explore machine-learning methods for physical
  problems, including generating and sampling states in topologically
  constrained systems.

For research details and publications, visit the
[Kao Group website](https://yingjerkao.gitlab.io).
